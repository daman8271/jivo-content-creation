## Who we're looking for

Two well-rounded **shoot-edit creators** — the kind of person who can walk into a kitchen, set up the shot, shoot a product/food reel that looks professional, and then cut it into a scroll-stopping piece the same day. You own the clip from idea → shoot → edit → ready-to-post.

Across the 2 hires we want full coverage of: **professional shooting**, **fast short-form editing**, and **trend/meme instinct**. It's fine if one of you skews slightly more "shooter" and the other slightly more "editor" — but **both must be able to shoot and edit**.

## What you'll SHOOT (must-have)

- **Product & food reels** — bottle/hero shots, pours, drizzles, tadka sizzles, kitchen action, "money shots".
- Professional **camera operation** (mirrorless/DSLR and/or iPhone Pro), proper **lighting** (softbox/natural), clean **framing for 9:16**, basic **audio** capture.
- On-set **food / product styling** sense — making oil, dishes and the bottle look appetizing and on-brand.
- Comfortable shooting **UGC / talking-head / creator-style** content (handheld, real, phone-camera feel) as well as polished cinematic b-roll.

## What you'll EDIT

1. **Fast-cut vertical Reels (9:16)** — the growth engine. Hard-cut, multi-beat pacing; "boom/impact" reveals on the key moment (punch + flash + shake, beat-locked). *Not* calm/premium slow pushes.
2. **Carousels (4:5)** — multi-slide Instagram carousels on a locked template; includes animated/motion carousels (kinetic type).
3. **Memes / trend-jacking** — fast, topical, caption-driven, Hinglish.
4. **Cinematic grade + captions** — light color grading, clean burned-in on-screen text and timing.

## We also work with AI (you don't have to be an expert — but be open)

Some of our footage and images are **AI-generated** (tools like Higgsfield, Seedance, Kling, Runway, GPT-Image). You'll sometimes blend real shot footage with AI clips: assembling segments, keeping the product **logo/label legible**, upscaling, and matching looks. Willingness and ability to learn this fast is a big plus.

Edible-oil brand — **no medical/health claims** ("cures/prevents/treats/guaranteed weight loss" etc.). Keep copy product-led and factual ("cold-pressed", "extra light", "high smoke point", "rich in MUFA"). When in doubt, flag it.
